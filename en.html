01 / ABOUT ME

About

My research areas are human–machine–AI collaboration and AI agent decision making — studying coordination mechanisms and intelligent task allocation in heterogeneous systems of humans, physical machines, and AI agents, and the problems of capability modeling, role configuration, and trustworthy collaboration that arise as AI evolves from an assistive tool into an autonomous decision maker. I am a Ph.D. candidate in Management Science and Engineering at Nanjing University (enrolled Sep 2023, expected graduation Jun 2027), trained in the Department of Control Science and Intelligence Engineering and ranked 1/24 in my program.

My methodology builds on multi-agent systems, role-based collaboration theory, and combinatorial optimization, drawing on mixed-integer programming, heuristic and hierarchical solvers, and reinforcement learning, and extending to LLM evaluation, post-training, and online AI agent decision making. Beyond academic work, I have contributed to applied research projects on intelligent decision making for complex systems, and I currently work on production LLM algorithms in the Tencent Xiaowei Agent core business.

Scope of this page: all publications, projects, awards, and grants listed here were produced after I began my Ph.D. in September 2023 (roughly a three-year window). Master’s and undergraduate work is not included.
